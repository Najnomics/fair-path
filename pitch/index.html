Fair Path is one pool with three corridors. Live on Unichain Sepolia, fpVOL/fpUSD.

The problem

MEV isn't magic — it's a pricing failure

  • Attestation-only can't tell a slot-0 searcher from retail that landed in an unattested block.
  • Delay / encryption punishes everyone and still leaks LVR.
  • Size / vol fees can't tell a searcher from a whale.
  • TOB LVR auctions sell who is first globally — not this pool's path, slot, or bond.

The gap

Fair Flow asked one boolean: was the block fair?

First-look inside the block, and capital at risk, were still free options on LPs.

Don't bash Fair Flow — position as the next question. Uniform pricing plus unpriced first-look.

The insight

Charge an honest price for attested flow — rent first-look as a slot — and send a bill (or a slash) to everyone else.

Same pool. Same liquidity. No fork, no dark pool, no FHE. The price of now depends on three facts.

Same pool Dynamic-fee override Flashblocks Searcher bond donate()
Three facts: who built the block, flashblock slot, bondedOf(searcher).

SearcherBond
